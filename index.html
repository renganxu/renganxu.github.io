About

Research shaped by
systems thinking.

I am a Member of Technical Staff at Microsoft AI (MAI) Superintelligence. Previously, I was a Research Scientist at Meta SuperIntelligence Labs. I focus on scaling reinforcement learning post-training for high throughput—spanning attention, MoE, grouped GEMM, parallelism strategies, and E2E system efficiency.
